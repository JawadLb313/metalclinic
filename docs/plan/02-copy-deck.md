## Programs (accordion, 5 items)
- **Eyebrow:** `WHAT WE RUN`
- **H2:** `THE PROGRAMS`
- **Sub:** "Five ways in. Same standards behind all of them."

**01 · TRANSFORMATION PROTOCOL** — *the flagship*
"Twelve weeks, structured and time-bound. Training, nutrition and weekly accountability in one block, built for people who want a decision made for them."
- Goal-oriented plan, rewritten at weeks 4 and 8
- Before-and-after measurements on record
- Weekly check-in plus chat access to your coach
- *For:* the restart. Desk job, health scare, or twenty years since you last trained.

**02 · ONE-TO-ONE PERSONAL TRAINING**
"Private sessions, entirely your programme. The fastest way to fix technique, because someone is watching every rep."
- Sessions built around your goal and your history
- Hands-on coaching, cueing and spotting
- Flexible scheduling around work
- *For:* anyone who wants full attention, or who's coming back from injury.

**03 · SMALL-GROUP STRENGTH**
"Two to six people, one coach, one programme. The accountability of a class with the correction of personal training."
- Capped groups so nobody trains unsupervised
- Barbell-focused: squat, hinge, press, pull, carry
- Lower cost per session than one-to-one
- *For:* the plateaued lifter, and anyone who trains better with company.

**04 · NUTRITION COACHING**
"Habit-based, Precision Nutrition method. No shakes, no detoxes, no plan that assumes you don't eat with your family on Sunday."
- One habit at a time, built to survive real life
- Food you already eat, in portions that work
- Available on its own or inside any training package
- *For:* people whose training is fine and whose kitchen isn't.

**05 · ONLINE COACHING**
"The full protocol, delivered anywhere. Programming, video form review, and a coach who answers."
- Programme delivered to your phone
- Video review — you film the set, you get notes back
- Scheduled check-in calls
- *For:* clients abroad, frequent travellers, or anyone whose gym isn't ours.
