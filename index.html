About the clinic

Built by someone who needed it first

Alain Eid spent eleven years behind a desk in banking. In 2006 a doctor told him his weight had become a health risk. He lost a hundred pounds — then spent the next decade learning exactly how it happened, so he could do it for other people on purpose.

He left banking in 2018, studied at the American Fitness and Nutrition Academy in Pasadena, passed the ACSM Elite Fitness Certification, and opened The Metal Clinic in September 2020.

How it works

Four steps. No guessing.

The same sequence for every client, whether you're here to lose forty kilos or add ten to your squat.

01

Assess

A movement screen, a readiness check, baseline measurements and an honest conversation about your schedule.

02

Prescribe

You leave with a written program built around your goal, the days you can actually train, and the injuries you actually have.

03

Execute

Coached sessions on the floor or online. Form corrected, lifts spotted, load progressed on a schedule instead of a mood.

04

Review

Your progress is measured regularly. If the numbers moved, we push. If they didn't, we change the plan — not your willpower.
